About The Art of Interior Designs

Passion for Beautiful and Authentic Spaces

We are based in Gaborone and Francistown, Botswana. The Art of Interior Designs has been transforming homes and offices since 2020 with innovative designs that balance aesthetics, comfort and sustainability. Our mission is to create spaces that tell your story and reflect the vibrant spirit of Botswana.
