Design Tips

The following are designs tips from our design experts:

  • Use accent walls to add personality and draw focus to key areas.
  • Layer different lighting sources for ambiance, task, and accent lighting.
  • Invest in quality furniture pieces that blend comfort with timeless style.
  • Create moments focus on creating small, intentional scenes, such as a styled reading corner or a curated vignette on a table.
  • Layer texture, combine materials like soft throws, velvet, wood, and natural stone to add depth to neutral rooms.
  • Create a focal point, give the eye a place to rest by highlighting a fireplace, artwork or a large mirro.
